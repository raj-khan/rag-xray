# Expense Policy

This policy explains what Orbita Labs reimburses and how to claim it.

## Submitting a claim

All expenses are submitted in the Ledgerly tool within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected automatically. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.

## Approval

Your direct manager approves every claim. Any single expense above 500 USD also needs approval from a director before it is reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Travel meals

The daily meal allowance is 45 USD for domestic travel and 70 USD for international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Flights and hotels

Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy. Hotels are capped at 180 USD per night, or 260 USD per night in high-cost cities such as London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich.

## Home office

Each employee has a one-time home office budget of 800 USD, available during the first 6 months of employment, for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment.
