# Leave Policy

Orbita Labs wants people to rest. This policy covers paid time off for all full-time employees.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Leave is requested in the Perch app at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences only need two working days of notice.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31. Any balance above 5 days is lost on January 1 and is not paid out.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for any sick absence longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

All parents, regardless of gender, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. It can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after a birth or adoption.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the country in which the employee's contract is held. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no replacement day is given.
