# Office Guide

Orbita Labs has one office, in Lisbon, and most people work remotely.

## Opening hours

The Lisbon office is open from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. It is closed on weekends and on Portuguese public holidays.

## Desk booking

Desks are booked in the Perch app, up to 14 days ahead. Bookings that are not checked into by 11:00 are released automatically.

## Visitors

Visitors must be registered in Perch at least one day in advance and must be accompanied by an employee at all times.

## Team offsites

Each team can hold one offsite per year. The company covers travel and accommodation following the expense policy, plus up to 150 USD per person per day for the venue and activities.
